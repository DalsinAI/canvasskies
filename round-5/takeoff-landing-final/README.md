# Take-off and landing films, after the art pass

What the game will show: each film decoded from `assets/canvas-skies/airfield-films.bin`
(320x200, 12 frames a second, 16 greys, doubled to 640x400), with its own
synthesised soundtrack (`*.wav`, 11025 Hz mono). `contact-sheet.png` is as decoded;
`contact-sheet-master.png` is the full-quality render before encoding.

Generated by `ports/amigaos3/films/` and `ports/amigaos3/make_airfield_films.py`.
In take-offs 1 and 4 the ground crew stand at least 5 m clear of the machine from
throttle-up on, checked frame by frame.
